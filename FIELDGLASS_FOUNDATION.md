# The Foundation of Fieldglass®

## Origin, Purpose, and Scientific Lineage

Fieldglass® was not created as a software application.

It emerged as the operational expression of a broader research program dedicated to understanding runtime behavior, identity continuity, failure formation, drift dynamics, and the observable structure of intelligence during active inference.

The project originates from the Recursive Science® research program developed by Arjay Asadi between 2024 and 2026.

Over a period of two years, this research evolved into a unified body of work consisting of thirteen foundational volumes, multiple standards documents, instrumentation frameworks, evidence schemas, and operational methodologies focused on a central question:

> Can runtime behavior itself become evidence?

Fieldglass® represents the first public attempt to answer that question.

---

# The Problem

Modern AI systems are primarily understood through two lenses:

## Benchmark Evaluation

Benchmarks measure capability.

Examples include:

* MMLU
* HumanEval
* SWE-Bench
* AgentBench

These frameworks answer:

> Can a model perform a task?

They do not explain how runtime behavior evolved during task execution.

---

## Operational Monitoring

Monitoring measures infrastructure health.

Examples include:

* Latency
* Throughput
* Cost
* Availability
* Error rates

These systems answer:

> Was the infrastructure functioning?

They do not explain how failures formed within the interaction itself.

---

Between benchmarking and monitoring exists a largely unobserved space:

```text
Runtime Behavior
```

Fieldglass® was created to make this space observable.

---

# Runtime Evidence™

The central premise behind Fieldglass® is that runtime behavior can be reconstructed, inspected, challenged, preserved, and treated as evidence.

Instead of viewing interactions as transient outputs, Runtime Evidence™ treats them as observable trajectories capable of producing:

* Warning signals
* Formation timelines
* Boundary crossings
* Drift patterns
* Role dynamics
* Pressure accumulation
* Recovery signatures
* Replayable evidence artifacts

The objective is not prediction.

The objective is reconstruction.

# The Fieldglass® Observatory

Fieldglass® is a browser-local Runtime Evidence Observatory.

Its purpose is to allow operators to:

```text
Ingest
→ Compute
→ Instrument
→ Analyze
→ Investigate
→ Export
→ Preserve
```

without requiring:

* Cloud processing
* Hidden telemetry
* Account creation
* Centralized storage

All computation occurs locally within the user's browser.
